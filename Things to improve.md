### Improvements I Would Make

**Glove choice**  
The only glove I had access to at the time was a tight football glove. While it allowed me to test finger movement, it was difficult to remove, and taking it off often damaged the aluminum foil strips. Using a looser glove or a custom fabric base would have reduced stress on the sensors.

**Sensor durability**  
The aluminum foil was fragile and prone to tearing. If I were to rebuild the hand, I would reinforce the foil with a thin layer of cardboard or another backing material to improve durability and maintain consistent spacing during movement.

**Electrical connections**  
At the time, I did not have access to soldering equipment, so I taped wires directly onto the aluminum foil. This worked temporarily but led to unreliable connections. Now that I have soldering tools, I would solder the connections to improve stability and reduce signal loss.

**Overall reflection**  
These limitations taught me how access to materials and tools can shape design decisions. Looking back, I better understand how small mechanical and electrical choices affect reliability in a system.
